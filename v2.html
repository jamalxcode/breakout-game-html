How to Play

Mouse: move to steer, click to launch and fire. Touch: drag anywhere on the screen, tap to launch and fire. Keys: ← → to steer, Space to launch and fire, P to pause.

MMulti-Ball: every ball splits in three
RRockets: tap to fire, they blast nearby bricks
ZLasers: tap or hold for rapid fire
BSuper Bomb: next hit blows up a huge area
FFireball: smashes straight through bricks
CCatch: the bat holds the ball so you can aim
WBig Bat: a wider paddle
SSlow Ball: slows every ball down
$Bonus: a cash prize up to 5,000

–Shrink: avoid! A tiny bat

Gold bricks pay 1,000. Mystery (?) bricks always drop a good power-up. Grey metal only breaks to a Super Bomb. Striped bricks explode. Stuck on the last few bricks? The ball starts homing in on them.
