How to Play

Move with the mouse, a finger or the ← → keys. Click, tap or press Space to launch. P or Esc pauses.

MMulti-Ball: splits every ball in three
RRocket Launchers: the bat fires rockets
BSuper Bomb: next hit blows up a huge area
$Bonus Points: a cash prize
WBig Bat: a wider paddle
SSlow Ball: slows every ball down
+Extra Life
–Shrink: watch out for this one

Bricks with dots take several hits. Grey metal bricks only break to a Super Bomb. Red explosive bricks blow up their neighbours. Break bricks in a row without touching the bat to build a combo.
